Elephant Math

The steal cap is the only thing between you and midnight.

Boundary: counting arithmetic only Elephant Math multiplies your guest count by your house rules. It never settles the dispute over who stole the air fryer - the table has its own courts.

The night truth

Twelve guests at 3 minutes a turn is a 36-minute game before the stealing starts. Schedule dessert after, not during.

The seat truth

Seat 1 shops blind; seat 12 sees all 11 opened gifts and shops the whole table. The draw decides the evening, and everyone pretends it does not.

The ceiling truth

With 12 gifts and a 3-steal cap, the stealing can run 36 steals before every gift freezes. Without the cap it runs until someone leaves.

Run your numbers
Elephant Math is exact arithmetic over your guest list and your house rules - turns, seats and steals, nothing more.
